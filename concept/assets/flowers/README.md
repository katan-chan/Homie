# Flower assets

Tạo bằng công cụ imagegen tích hợp, nền PNG trong suốt. Hai ảnh là hình tạo bởi AI dựa trên loài thật, không phải ảnh chụp thực vật được xác minh. Không dùng SVG. Giữ alpha gốc; chỉ co giãn và ghép ảnh trong canvas của concept.

## meadow-daisies-cosmos.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent PNG flower element for the side of a romantic personal garden website. Create one airy upright wildflower cluster of botanically recognizable real species: white oxeye daisies (Leucanthemum vulgare), soft pale pink Cosmos bipinnatus with delicate feathered foliage, a few tiny blue cornflowers (Centaurea cyanus). Natural slender stems and irregular small leaves, blossoms at different heights. Photoreal botanical cutout, very fine petal detail, softly diffused natural daylight, gentle blush/ivory/periwinkle colors and muted sage greenery. Portrait composition, narrow cluster, generous actual transparent space around edges, entire stems and flowers visible, no cut-off flower heads. Flowers should feel organically growing from a garden, not a florist bouquet; no pot, vase, ribbon, ground, background, text, watermark, illustration, SVG/vector/flat icon appearance, neon colors, or hard shadows. Genuinely transparent background, preserve alpha.

## meadow-ranunculus-bellflowers.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent PNG botanical flower element for the opposite side of a soft romantic garden website. Create one airy upright growing cluster of real identifiable flowers: ivory and pale peach Ranunculus asiaticus with many concentric paper-thin petals, a few pale lavender bellflowers (Campanula persicifolia) with true open bell-shaped flowers along slender stems, and small soft yellow California poppies (Eschscholzia californica) with natural four-petal cups and fine blue-green foliage. Match natural detailed botanical photography, soft diffuse morning light, sage green stems, low saturation pastel tones. No saturated orange or neon. Portrait composition, narrow asymmetric cluster, different heights, fine stems visible and organically growing, generous transparent space, whole flower heads and stems intact, no framing or background. Truly transparent alpha background. No vase, pot, bouquet tie, soil, text, watermark, SVG, vector, flat illustration or icons. Web decorative cutout, natural petal textures.
